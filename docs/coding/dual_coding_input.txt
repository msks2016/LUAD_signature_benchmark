### ⚠️ Critical Notes

1. **No independent external validation** — All analysis was performed in the same TCGA-LUAD cohort. The authors themselves acknowledge: "the data source of this study is single, and the amount of data included is not large, so the analysis results may have certain deviation" and "the prognostic model of our established model is required to be further confirmed in other independent cohorts."
2. **Coefficients provided** — Formula explicitly stated in the text with all 9 coefficients.
3. **Expression scale specified** — RNA-seq data from TCGA; log2-transformed.
4. **Events not reported** — Number of deaths is not stated in the paper.
5. **Actual cutoff value not reported** — only "median" is stated.
6. **C-index reported** — **0.759** (95% CI: 0.710–0.808).
7. **AUC at 1, 3, 5 years** — Reported (0.757, 0.721, 0.735).
8. **HR with 95% CI reported** — For risk score in univariate (1.507, 1.368–1.660) and multivariate (1.434, 1.294–1.591) analyses.
9. **No experimental validation** — The authors acknowledge: "functional experiments should be conducted to further indicate the potential molecular mechanisms."
10. **Annotation version not specified** — Unlike Paper #91, this paper does not state which GENCODE version was used.
11. **LncRNA name stability** — Names like LINC01150, AC010980.2, AL606489.1, AL034397.3, LINC00460, LINC02081, FAM83A-AS1, AL365181.2, and AC026355.1 may change between annotation versions.

### ⚠️ Critical Notes

1. **No independent external validation** — All validation cohorts are random splits of the same TCGA-LUAD dataset. This is a **major contamination risk** if you plan to validate this signature on TCGA-LUAD data. You would need a completely separate dataset (e.g., GEO) for true independent validation. The authors themselves state: "a major limitation of our study is that our findings are based on bioinformatics analysis, and will have to be validated on cross-cohort samples."
2. **Annotation version not specified** — Unlike Paper #91, this paper does not state which GENCODE version was used.
3. **Coefficients provided** — Formula explicitly stated in the text.
4. **Events not reported** — Number of deaths is not stated in the paper.
5. **Actual cutoff value not reported** — only "median" is stated.
6. **C-index not reported**.
7. **Exact AUC values not reported** — ROC curves are shown in Figure 4, but the numerical AUC values are not provided in the text.
8. **HR/CI not reported** — The paper states the risk score was an independent prognostic factor, but does not provide the actual HR and 95% CI values.
9. **Experimental validation present** — siRNA knockdown of LINC00628 in NCI-H460 and NCI-H1299 cell lines, with Transwell and wound healing assays showing reduced migration and invasion.
10. **LncRNA name stability** — Names like LINC00628, LINC02147, and MIR34AHG may change between annotation versions.
