### ⚠️ Critical Notes

1. **No independent external validation cohort** — All bioinformatic analysis was performed in the same TCGA-LUAD cohort. The authors themselves acknowledge: "the prognosis prediction model was just constructed based on TCGA datasets, while validation by external dataset was lacking."
2. **Coefficients provided** — Table 1 provides all 7 coefficients.
3. **Expression scale not explicitly stated** — RNA-seq data from TCGA; specific scale (FPKM/TPM/counts) not stated.
4. **Annotation version not specified** — Unlike some papers, this paper does not state which GENCODE or Ensembl version was used.
5. **Events not reported** — Number of deaths is not stated in the paper.
6. **Actual cutoff value not reported** — only "median risk score" is stated.
7. **C-index not reported**.
8. **AUC at 1, 3, 5 years** — Reported for training (0.740, 0.682, 0.650), validation (0.665, 0.663, 0.652), and entire (0.699, 0.686, 0.647).
9. **HR with 95% CI for risk score** — Not numerically stated in the text (only p < 0.001 in Figure 4A-C); Table 1 provides HR for individual lncRNAs.
10. **External validation** — None; internal TCGA validation set only (n=252); experimental validation in 20 paired LUAD tissues and cell lines.
11. **Population** — TCGA-LUAD; subgroups analysed by age, gender, stage, T, N, M.
12. **Sample size** — 504 patients (252 training + 252 validation); 20 paired LUAD tissues and adjacent normal tissues.
13. **Hypoxia-related genes** — 200 genes from MSigDB hallmark-hypoxia gene set.
14. **HRlncRNAs** — 221 differentially expressed hypoxia-related lncRNAs (adjusted P < 0.05, |logFC| ≥ 1); 79 prognostic HRlncRNAs from univariate Cox; 7 from LASSO and multivariate Cox.
15. **Consensus clustering** — 3 hypoxia-related clusters (C1 n=195, C2 n=187, C3 n=118); C2 had best prognosis, C1 had worst OS (P < 0.001).
16. **Immune analysis** — High-risk group had higher neutrophils, M0 macrophages, M2 macrophages; low-risk group had higher monocytes; high-risk group had higher expression of immune checkpoints (B7-H3, LAG3, PD-1, PD-L1).
17. **GSEA** — High-risk group enriched in glycolysis, hypoxia, EMT, PI3K-AKT-MTOR signaling, apoptosis, angiogenesis.
18. **LINC00941** — Risk factor; promotes LUAD progression and angiogenesis; also promotes pancreatic adenocarcinoma, colon cancer, papillary thyroid cancer, gastric cancer.
19. **LINC00707** — Risk factor; miR-876 sponge in breast cancer; regulates cisplatin sensitivity in NSCLC via miR-145.
20. **AL161431.1** — Risk factor; oncogene in endometrial carcinoma via MAPK; predicts poor OS in LUSC; upregulated under hypoxia; knockdown suppresses proliferation, migration, and induces apoptosis in A549 cells; targets miR-1252-5p.
21. **AC090001.1** — Protective factor; not previously reported in any cancers.
22. **AC022784.1** — Risk factor; previously identified in LUAD risk signature (You et al., 2021).
23. **AC079949.2** — Risk factor; previously identified in LUAD risk signature (Li et al., 2020).
24. **AC010980.2** — Risk factor; previously identified in LUAD risk signature (Wu et al., 2021).
25. **AL161431.1** — Also appears in Paper #330 (Cuproptosis-Related lncRNA Signature) and Paper #82 (Cuproptosis-Related lncRNA Signature) as a risk gene.
26. **AC026355.2** — Not in this paper's model, but appears in Papers #213, #327, #181, #245, #190, and #295, suggesting it is a recurrent lncRNA in LUAD prognostic models.
27. **LINC01116** — Appears in Paper #213 (Anoikis-Related lncRNA Signature) and Paper #250 (Genome Instability-Related lncRNA Signature) as a risk gene.
28. **FAM83A-AS1** — Appears in Paper #250 (Genome Instability-Related lncRNA Signature) and Paper #369 (Necroptosis-Related lncRNA Signature) as a risk gene.
