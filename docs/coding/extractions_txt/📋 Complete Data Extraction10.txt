### ⚠️ Critical Notes

1. **No independent external validation cohort** — All bioinformatic analysis was performed in the same TCGA-LUAD cohort. The authors themselves acknowledge: "owing to the limited number of LUAD samples in the TCGA database, a larger dataset needs to be included to validate our m6A/m1A/A-to-I/APA modification writer-related lncRNA model."
2. **Coefficients provided** — Table 1 and text provide all 7 coefficients.
3. **Expression scale explicitly stated** — FPKM; average FPKM < 0.1 excluded.
4. **Annotation version explicitly stated** — GENCODE (https://www.gencodegenes.org/human/).
5. **Events not reported** — Number of deaths is not stated in the paper.
6. **Actual cutoff value not reported** — only "median risk score" is stated.
7. **C-index not reported**.
8. **AUC at 1 year only** — Reported (0.712 training, 0.679 testing); 3-year and 5-year AUC not reported.
9. **HR with 95% CI** — Numerically stated in Figure 4I,J (training) and Figure 4K,L (testing): training univariate 1.33 (1.23–1.44), training multivariate 1.34 (1.23–1.46), testing univariate 1.36 (1.25–1.48), testing multivariate 1.36 (1.24–1.49).
10. **External validation** — None; internal TCGA testing set only (n=252); experimental validation via qPCR in paired LUAD patient samples.
11. **Population** — TCGA-LUAD; subgroups analysed by sex, stage, T, N, M.
12. **Sample size** — 504 patients (252 training + 252 testing); 504 LUAD + 58 normal samples from TCGA.
13. **RNA modification writers** — 29 writers analysed: 8 m6A (METTL3, METTL14, METTL16, WTAP, KIAA1429, RBM15, RBM15B, ZC3H13), 6 m1A (TRMT6, TRMT61A, TRMT61B, TRMT10C, BMT2, RRP8), 12 APA (PCF11, CFI, CLP1, NUDT21, PABPN1, CPSF1/2/3/4, CSTF1/2/3), 3 A-to-I (ADAR, ADARB1, ADARB2).
14. **Consensus clustering** — 2 molecular subtypes (Cluster 1 and Cluster 2) based on 15 prognostic lncRNAs; Cluster 1 had worse OS than Cluster 2 (p = 0.013); Cluster 1 mostly men and patients with lymph node metastases; Cluster 2 mostly women and patients without lymph node metastases.
15. **Immune analysis** — Low-risk group had higher immune scores, stromal scores, and ESTIMATE scores; high-risk group had higher infiltration of CD8+ T cells, CD4+ activated memory T cells, M0 macrophages, and activated mast cells; low-risk group had higher infiltration of resting CD4+ memory T cells, monocytes, resting dendritic cells, and resting mast cells.
16. **Immune checkpoint genes** — 15 genes differentially expressed: HHLA2, TNFSF14, VSIR, CD27, CD40LG, NCR3, BTLA, ENTPD1, CD40, CD274, CTLA4, ICOS, TMIGD2, TNFRSF4 highly expressed in low-risk group; CD276 highly expressed in high-risk group.
17. **TMB analysis** — High-risk group had higher TMB; TP53 highest mutation rate in both subgroups.
18. **TIDE analysis** — Low-risk subgroup had higher TIDE score (less sensitive to immunotherapy); high-risk group had lower TIDE score (more sensitive to immunotherapy).
19. **IPS analysis** — Low-risk group responded better to anti-PD-1 treatment (p = 0.013); high-risk group responded better to combination treatment with anti-PD-1 and CTLA-4 blockade (p = 0.028).
20. **Drug sensitivity** — Low-risk group more sensitive to osimertinib, ABT-888, AP-24534, AS601245, ATRA; high-risk group more sensitive to A-443654, A-770041, AG-014699, AUY922, AZ628, AZD-0530.
21. **Stemness index** — LINC01352, LINC00968, AC024075.1, AC005070.3, AL133445.2, AC005856.1 negatively correlated with RNAss; LINC01352, LINC00968, AL133445.2, AC005856.1 negatively correlated with DNAss.
22. **AC092168.2** — Also in Wu et al. immune-related prognostic signature (Paper #294 reference 36).
23. **LINC01352** — Also in ferroptosis-related lncRNA prognostic signature (Record et al., Paper #294 reference 39).
24. **LINC00968** — Inhibits LUAD progression; attenuates drug resistance in breast cancer; promotes epithelial ovarian cancer and osteosarcoma.
25. **AC026355.2** — Not in this paper's model, but appears in Papers #213, #327, #181, and #245, suggesting it is a recurrent lncRNA in LUAD prognostic models.
26. **LINC01116** — Appears in Paper #213 (Anoikis-Related lncRNA Signature) and Paper #250 (Genome Instability-Related lncRNA Signature) as a risk gene.

### ⚠️ Critical Notes

1. **No independent external validation cohort** — All bioinformatic analysis was performed in the same TCGA-LUAD cohort. The authors themselves acknowledge: "most of the approaches in this study utilized bioinformatics analysis; hence, the conclusions need to be further validated experimentally in vitro and in vivo."
2. **Coefficients provided** — Table S4 provides all 6 coefficients.
3. **Expression scale not explicitly stated** — RNA-seq data from TCGA; specific scale (FPKM/TPM/counts) not stated.
4. **Annotation version explicitly stated** — GENCODE.
5. **Events not reported** — Number of deaths is not stated in the paper.
6. **Actual cutoff value not reported** — only "optimal cut-off value" is stated.
7. **C-index shown graphically** — Figure 6K shows nomogram had highest C-index but value not numerically stated.
8. **AUC at 1, 3, 5 years** — Reported for CRLS (0.708, 0.666, 0.616) and nomogram (0.843, 0.757, 0.766).
9. **HR with 95% CI** — Numerically stated in Figure 6A-F: training univariate 1.376 (1.203–1.575), training multivariate 1.318 (1.082–1.606), testing univariate 1.099 (1.025–1.179), testing multivariate 1.092 (1.009–1.181), entire univariate 1.154 (1.090–1.221), entire multivariate 1.131 (1.061–1.205).
10. **External validation** — None; internal TCGA testing set only (n=244); experimental validation in LUAD cell lines.
11. **Population** — TCGA-LUAD; subgroups analysed by age, gender, stage, T, N, M.
12. **Sample size** — 490 patients (246 training + 244 testing); 535 tumor + 59 normal samples from TCGA.
13. **Cuproptosis-related genes** — 10 CRGs from literature (FDX1, LIAS, LIPT1, DLD, DLAT, PDHA1, PDHB, MTF1, GLS, CDKN2A).
14. **CRLs** — 852 cuproptosis-related lncRNAs identified (Pearson correlation coefficient > 0.4, p < 0.001); 6 from univariate, LASSO, and multivariate Cox regression.
15. **Immune analysis** — Low CRLS group had higher infiltration of B cells, iDCs, Mast cells, NK cells; higher HLA and type II IFN response; higher expression of immune checkpoint members (CD28, BTLA, BTNL2, CD40, TNFSF14, TNFSF9); higher IPS (better immunotherapy response).
16. **TMB analysis** — High CRLS group had higher TMB (93.15% vs 83.19%); positive correlation between CRLS and TMB (R = 0.15, P = 0.001); low CRLS + high TMB had best prognosis.
17. **Drug sensitivity** — High CRLS group more sensitive to erlotinib, gefitinib, cisplatin, paclitaxel, gemcitabine; 7 compounds for high CRLS (A.443654, A.770041, AG.014699, AICAR, AKT.inhibitor.VIII, AUY922, AZD.0530); 3 compounds for low CRLS (AS601245, ATRA, Axitinib).
18. **GO/KEGG** — DEGs enriched in immune-related biological processes (antimicrobial humoral response, antibacterial humoral response, humoral immune response, negative regulation of proteolysis); KEGG: PI3K-Akt, MAPK, ECM-receptor interaction, IL-17 signaling, drug metabolism-cytochrome P450, retinol metabolism; DO: lung disease, NSCLC.
19. **GSEA** — High CRLS group enriched in cell cycle, DNA replication, homologous recombination, mismatch repair, P53 signaling; low CRLS group enriched in arachidonic acid metabolism, fatty acid metabolism, propanoate metabolism.
20. **AC090541.1** — Risk factor; downregulated in LUAD cells.
21. **AC009226.1** — Risk factor; upregulated in LUAD cells.
22. **AC027097.2** — Protective factor; downregulated in LUAD cells.
23. **AC026355.2** — Protective factor; also identified as immune-related and autophagy-related lncRNA in LUAD; appears in Papers #213, #327, #181, #245, and #190, suggesting it is a recurrent lncRNA in LUAD prognostic models.
24. **NIFK-AS1** — Protective factor; downregulated in LUAD cells; suppresses proliferation and migration of LUAD cells; also up-regulated in HCC and promotes HCC progression by m6A methylation; suppresses M2 polarization of macrophages in endometrial cancer via miR-146a; serves as a signature related to OS in glioblastoma.
25. **AC106028.2** — Protective factor; downregulated in A549, upregulated in H1975.
26. **AC026355.2** — Also appears in Papers #213 (Anoikis-Related lncRNA Signature), #327 (Glutathione Metabolism-Associated lncRNA Signature), #181 (Exosome-Related lncRNA Signature), #245 (Pyroptosis-Related lncRNA Signature), and #190 (Cuproptosis-Related lncRNA Signature).
27. **LINC01116** — Appears in Paper #213 (Anoikis-Related lncRNA Signature) and Paper #250 (Genome Instability-Related lncRNA Signature) as a risk gene.
28. **FAM83A-AS1** — Appears in Paper #250 (Genome Instability-Related lncRNA Signature) and Paper #369 (Necroptosis-Related lncRNA Signature) as a risk gene.
