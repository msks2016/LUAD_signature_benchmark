1. **No independent external validation** — All validation is internal TCGA splits. The authors themselves acknowledge: "First, more external data should be considered to assess whether the LUADSenLncSig model fully matches the additional dataset."

### ⚠️ Critical Notes

1. **No independent external validation** — All validation is internal TCGA splits. The authors themselves acknowledge: "we mainly use TCGA database internal validation and lacked external validation to assess the applicability of predictive signatures."
2. **Coefficients provided** — Formula explicitly stated in the text with all 18 coefficients.
3. **Expression scale not explicitly stated** — RNA-seq data from TCGA; specific scale (FPKM/TPM/counts) not stated.
4. **Events not reported** — Number of deaths is not stated in the paper.
5. **Actual cutoff value not reported** — only "median" is stated.
6. **C-index not reported**.
7. **AUC at 1, 3, 5 years** — Reported for entire cohort and two internal validation cohorts.
8. **HR with 95% CI reported** — For risk score in univariate and multivariate analyses (approximately 1.028).
9. **Immune analysis** — ssGSEA and immune checkpoint analysis performed.
10. **TMB analysis** — Performed.
11. **Drug sensitivity analysis** — Multiple drugs tested.
12. **DFS prediction** — Separate 4-lncRNA signature for DFS constructed (AC127502.2, LINC00511, AL139289.2, UBE2Q1-AS1).
13. **SNP analysis** — LINC00996 and AF186192.1 analyzed using TRlnc and lncRNASNP.
14. **Transcription factor analysis** — LINC00996 and AF186192.1 regulated by TP53 and TP63.
15. **ceRNA network** — FAM30A and mRNA form a ceRNA network.
16. **No experimental validation** — The authors acknowledge: "the mechanism of methylation-related lncRNA in LUAD needs to be verified by clinical experiments."
17. **Annotation version not specified** — Unlike Paper #91, this paper does not state which GENCODE version was used.
18. **LncRNA name stability** — Names like AP002761.1, AL118558.3, CH17-340M24.3, AL353150.1, AC004687.1, LINC00996, AF186192.1, HSPC324, AC087752.3, FAM30A, AC106047.1, AC026355.1, ABALON, LINC01843, AL606489.1, NKILA, AP001453.2, and GSEC may change between annotation versions.
