### ⚠️ Critical Notes

1. **No independent external validation** — All analysis was performed in the same TCGA-LUAD cohort. The authors themselves acknowledge: "The lack of suitable lncRNA probes has limited external validation, which we plan to address with RNA-seq in clinical samples."
2. **Coefficients not numerically stated** — The formula is described but the actual regression coefficients for each lncRNA are **not provided** in the text. You would need to extract them from Supplementary Table 3 or contact the authors.
3. **Formula explicitly written** — Risk score = Σ(αi × βi), where α = regression coefficient, β = expression value; no intercept.
4. **Expression scale explicitly stated** — RNA-seq data from TCGA; **log2 transformation** applied before analysis.
5. **Annotation version explicitly stated** — Ensembl human genome browser **GRCh38**.
6. **Events not reported** — Number of deaths is not stated in the paper.
7. **Actual cutoff value not reported** — only "median risk score" is stated.
8. **C-index not numerically stated** — Shown in Figure 4E but values not in text.
9. **AUC at 1, 3, 5 years** — Reported (0.741, 0.684, 0.690).
10. **HR with 95% CI** — Numerically stated in Figure 4A,B: univariate 1.271 (1.215–1.330), multivariate 1.271 (1.200–1.317).
11. **Experimental validation** — ✅ Yes (qRT-PCR, CCK8, colony formation, wound healing, invasion assay, xenograft mouse model).
12. **Population** — TCGA-LUAD; subgroups analysed by age, gender, stage, early/late stage.
13. **Sample size** — 507 patients (after excluding 10 with incomplete clinical information); 355 training + 152 testing.
14. **GSH-related gene set** — 87 genes identified (including ABCC1, ABCC4, ABCC5, etc.); 1,748 lncRNAs associated with GSH metabolism (Pearson correlation > 0.4, p < 0.001).
15. **TMB analysis** — High-risk group had higher TMB; TP53, KRAS, MUC16, COL11A1 mutations; high TMB + low-risk group had best survival; low TMB + high-risk group had worst survival.
