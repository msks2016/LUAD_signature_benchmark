### ⚠️ Critical Notes

1. **No independent external validation** — All validation cohorts are internal splits of the same TCGA-LUAD dataset. The authors themselves acknowledge: "We only used test and validation sets from TCGA database to verify the effectiveness of the lncRNA prognostic model associated with ferroptosis. Relevant basic experiments to detect the expression levels of the identified ferroptosis-associated lncRNAs in cell lines and clinical samples are scarce."
2. **Coefficients provided** — Table 2 provides coefficients for all 10 lncRNAs, and the full formula is written out in the text.
3. **Annotation version specified** — **GENCODE Release 29 (GRCh38.p12)** — this is a strength compared to most other papers.
4. **Events reported** — **153 dead / 290 alive** (from Table 1).
5. **C-index reported** — **0.763** (training) and **0.736** (validation) — a strength compared to most other papers.
6. **Time-specific AUC not reported** — Only overall AUC (0.741 training, 0.771 validation) is provided. No 1-year, 3-year, or 5-year AUC values.
7. **HR for risk score not reported** — Although the risk score was an independent predictor, the actual HR and 95% CI for the risk score are not provided in the text (only for individual lncRNAs in Table 2).
8. **Patients removed** — OS ≤ 30 days excluded (n=13); incomplete clinicopathological characteristics excluded (n=30).
9. **No experimental validation** — No qRT-PCR or cell line experiments. The authors acknowledge this as a limitation.
10. **LncRNA name stability** — Names like AC026355.1, AC124045.1, AC091185.1, AC027031.2, AL021707.2, AL031667.3, AL606834.1, and AC025048.4 may change between annotation versions.

### ⚠️ Critical Notes

1. **No independent external validation** — All analysis was performed in the same TCGA-LUAD cohort. The authors themselves acknowledge: "the small number of patients included in our study indicates that the results may not be extrapolatable to a larger population" and "a prospective study is needed to test the availability of the established prognostic model."
2. **No experimental validation** — The authors explicitly state: "we did not run an experiment to quantify the expression of lncRNA or test the potential signaling pathways, indicating the need for solid verification measures."
3. **Coefficients provided** — Table 1 provides coefficients for all 5 lncRNAs, and the full formula is written out in the text.
4. **Specific cutoff value provided** — **Median risk score = 0.9939** (a strength compared to most other papers).
5. **AUC at 1, 3, 5, 7 years** — Reported (0.735, 0.672, 0.662, 0.732), which is a strength.
6. **HR with 95% CI reported** — For risk score in univariate (1.075, 1.046–1.104) and multivariate (1.088, 1.057–1.120) analyses.
7. **C-index mentioned but value not reported** — The paper states the C-index was calculated but does not provide the actual value.
8. **Events not reported** — Number of deaths is not stated in the paper.
9. **Drug sensitivity analysis** — Performed for 5 drugs (cisplatin, gemcitabine, paclitaxel, gefitinib, erlotinib).
10. **Multi-omics validation** — GEPIA and HPA databases used to validate correlated genes (CFLAR, GAPDH, MLST8).
11. **Annotation version not specified** — Unlike Paper #91, this paper does not state which GENCODE version was used.
12. **LncRNA name stability** — Names like AL691432.2, AL606489.1, and HLA-DQB1-AS1 may change between annotation versions.
