2. **Coefficients provided** — Formula explicitly stated in the text with all 4 coefficients.
3. **Expression scale not explicitly stated** — RNA-seq data from TCGA; specific scale (FPKM/TPM/counts) not stated.
4. **Events reported** — 104 deaths (35.37%); 190 alive (64.63%).
5. **Actual cutoff value not reported** — only "median risk score" is stated.
6. **C-index not numerically stated** — Shown in Figure 8A but values not in text.
7. **AUC at 1, 3, 5 years** — Reported (0.746, 0.697, 0.692).
8. **HR with 95% CI** — Numerically stated in Figure 6A,B: univariate 1.056 (1.042–1.090), multivariate 1.061 (1.034–1.089).
9. **IMvigor210 validation** — Used for immunotherapy response analysis in urothelial bladder cancer (UBC), **not** as an independent LUAD validation cohort.
10. **No experimental validation** — No qRT-PCR or cell line experiments.
11. **Annotation version not specified** — Unlike some papers, this paper does not state which GENCODE or Ensembl version was used.
12. **Population** — TCGA-LUAD; subgroups analysed by age, gender, race, stage, T, N, M.
13. **Sample size** — 494 patients (330 training + 164 testing); 504 initial samples (494 tumors + 10 normal).
14. **Exosome-related lncRNA sources** — ExoBCD, ExoCarta, exoRBase, GSEA, GeneCards; 340 ExolncRNAs procured after eliminating duplicates.
15. **TMB analysis** — No statistically significant difference in TMB scores between high- and low-risk groups; TMB + risk score survival analysis performed.
16. **Stemness index (mRNAsi)** — Higher in tumor vs normal, M1 vs M0, males vs females; no significant difference in survival between high and low mRNAsi.
17. **Drug sensitivity** — High-risk group more sensitive to cisplatin, docetaxel, gemcitabine, paclitaxel, pazopanib, sorafenib, sunitinib; low-risk group more sensitive to metformin.

### ⚠️ Critical Notes

1. **Independent external validation** — ✅ Yes, in GEO meta-cohort (GSE31210, GSE37745, GSE50081; n=458). This is a strength compared to many other papers.
2. **Coefficients not numerically stated** — Figure 2D shows HR values from stepwise multivariate Cox regression but the actual regression coefficients are **not provided** in the text. You would need to extract them from Supplementary Material or contact the authors.
3. **Expression scale not explicitly stated** — RNA-seq data from TCGA; specific scale (FPKM/TPM/counts) not stated.
4. **Annotation version not specified** — Unlike some papers, this paper does not state which GENCODE or Ensembl version was used.
5. **Events not reported** — Number of deaths is not stated in the paper.
6. **Actual cutoff value not reported** — only "median risk score" is stated.
7. **C-index not reported**.
8. **AUC at 1, 3, 5 years** — Reported for TCGA (0.778, 0.768, 0.731) and GEO (0.765, 0.751, 0.709).
9. **HR with 95% CI** — Numerically stated in Figure 4A,B (TCGA) and Figure 5E,F (GEO): TCGA univariate 2.718 (2.179–3.391), TCGA multivariate 2.367 (1.892–2.963), GEO univariate 1.468 (1.331–1.620), GEO multivariate 1.398 (1.258–1.553).
10. **External validation** — GEO meta-cohort (GSE31210, GSE37745, GSE50081; n=458).
11. **Population** — TCGA-LUAD; subgroups analysed by age, gender, stage.
12. **Sample size** — 472 patients (TCGA); 458 patients (GEO meta-cohort); 526 tumor + 59 normal samples from TCGA.
13. **Cuproptosis-related genes** — 17 genes identified (ATP7A, ATP7B, CDKN2A, DBT, DLAT, DLD, DLST, FDX1, GCSH, GLS, LIAS, LIPT1, MTF1, PDHA1, PDHB, SLC31A1, LIPT2).
14. **CuRLs** — 201 CuRLs identified (Pearson correlation coefficient > 0.3, P < 0.001); 36 prognostic CuRLs from univariate Cox; 21 from LASSO; 10 from stepwise multivariate Cox.
15. **Immune analysis** — Low-risk group had higher immune cell infiltration (B cells, CD4+ T cells, CD8+ T cells, monocytes, activated myeloid dendritic cells); risk score negatively related to immune and stroma scores (xCELL); 36 of 79 ICGs differentially expressed; CTLA4 higher in low-risk; CD276 higher in high-risk; HLA class II genes higher in low-risk.
16. **TMB analysis** — High-risk group had higher TMB; high TMB associated with favorable prognosis; low TMB + high risk had worst OS; high TMB + low risk had best prognosis.
17. **Drug sensitivity** — Low-risk group more sensitive to cisplatin, docetaxel, gemcitabine, gefitinib, paclitaxel; low-risk group may benefit more from imatinib; 15 drugs showed significant differences between risk groups.
18. **GSVA** — Glycolysis, p53 pathway, MTORC1 signaling, apoptosis differentially enriched; low-risk group enriched for inflammatory response and angiogenesis.
19. **GSEA** — CuRLs enriched in cell death and immunity pathways (apoptosis, ferroptosis, necroptosis, PD-L1 expression, PD-1 checkpoint pathway, T cell receptor signaling, antigen processing and presenting).
20. **GO/KEGG** — 10 CuRL-related mRNAs enriched in immune receptor activity, T cell activation, T cell receptor signaling pathway.
21. **LINC01137** — Risk factor (HR 1.335, P=0.005); also identified as poor prognostic factor.
22. **TMPO-AS1** — Risk factor (HR 1.533, P<0.001); strongest risk factor.
23. **SEPSECS-AS1** — Protective factor (HR 0.672, P=0.023).
24. **CARD8-AS1** — Protective factor (HR 0.732, P=0.038).
25. **ZSCAN16-AS1** — Protective factor (HR 0.768, P=0.046).
26. **MIR31HG** — Risk factor (HR 1.275, P=0.055); borderline significance.
27. **DLGAP1-AS1** — Risk factor (HR 1.169, P=0.126); not statistically significant.
28. **APTR** — Protective factor (HR 0.805, P=0.072); not statistically significant.
29. **ELOA-AS1** — Protective factor (HR 0.666, P=0.100); not statistically significant.
30. **RUNDC3A-AS1** — Protective factor (HR 0.860, P=0.192); not statistically significant.
31. **AC026355.2** — Not in this paper's model, but appears in Papers #213, #327, #181, and #245, suggesting it is a recurrent lncRNA in LUAD prognostic models.
32. **LINC01116** — Appears in Paper #213 (Anoikis-Related lncRNA Signature) and Paper #250 (Genome Instability-Related lncRNA Signature) as a risk gene.
