**Key Takeaway:** Paper #196 provides **full coefficients**, **time-specific AUC values (1, 3, 5 years)**, **HR with 95% CI for the risk score (univariate and multivariate)**, **extensive immune analysis** (ESTIMATE, CIBERSORT, ssGSEA), **TMB analysis**, **TIDE immunotherapy prediction**, **drug sensitivity analysis** (GDSC/oncoPredict), and **GO/KEGG/GSEA enrichment**. The main limitations are the **lack of independent external validation**, **no C-index**, **no specific cutoff value**, **events not reported**, and **no experimental validation**. The authors themselves acknowledge the need for independent cohort validation and mechanistic experiments. The comprehensive performance metrics (especially the external validation attempt and experimental validation in other papers) make it a valuable candidate for sanity checks when re-computing the signature.
