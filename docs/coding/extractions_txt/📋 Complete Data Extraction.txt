### 3. Risk Score Formula

From the Methods section:

**Risk score = coef(lncRNA1) × expr(lncRNA1) + coef(lncRNA2) × expr(lncRNA2) + … + coef(lncRNAn) × expr(lncRNAn)**

Where:
- **coef** = coefficient from multivariate Cox regression (Table 2)
- **expr** = expression level of the lncRNA

**Full formula with coefficients:**

```
Risk score = (−0.141 × GAS6-AS1)
           + (−0.280 × AC106047.1)
           + (0.181 × AC010980.2)
           + (−0.186 × AL034397.3)
           + (0.080 × NKILA)
           + (0.167 × AL606489.1)
           + (−0.043 × HLA-DQB1-AS1)
           + (0.061 × LINC01116)
           + (−0.167 × LINC01806)
           + (0.031 × FAM83A-AS1)
           + (−0.142 × AC090559.1)
```

**Formula form:** Plain sum of coefficient × expression. **No intercept** is included.

3. **Coefficients provided** — Table 2.
