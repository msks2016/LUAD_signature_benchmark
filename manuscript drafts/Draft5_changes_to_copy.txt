2. Methods > Reporting completeness of the literature
Starts with (in your copy): "Five items were coded from the 76 papers..."
New text:
Five items were coded from the 76 papers, among the 90 full texts reviewed, for which detailed extraction records were available: a full coefficient table; whether the numeric risk cutoff was stated; whether the expression scale was stated (named unit), implied (RNA-seq mentioned without a unit) or not stated; independent external validation; and a conflict between the text (including the risk-score formula) and the tables in a coefficient's sign, a coefficient's value beyond rounding of the same value, or the number of genes or samples. The primary investigator coded all papers. A second investigator independently coded 10 randomly drawn papers on all items and blindly re-coded the text–table item in the other 66 papers; disagreements were resolved by re-reading the papers together (Updates 2 and 3; Supplementary File 1). Each paper was matched to its PubMed record, and the bibliographic details and codes of every paper are given in Supplementary Table S19.

4. Results > Reporting completeness
Starts with (in your copy): "Of the 76 reviewed papers..."
New text:
Of the 76 reviewed papers, 63 (82.9%) gave a full coefficient table. All described how the risk cutoff was chosen, but only 6 (7.9%) stated its value. The expression scale was stated with a named unit in 27 (35.5%), implied by a reference to RNA-seq in 38 (50.0%) and not stated in 11 (14.5%). Independent external validation was reported in 24 (31.6%), and the text and tables conflicted in 2 (2.6%), once in a coefficient's sign and once in its value (Supplementary Table S19). Before discussion, the two coders agreed on all 10 dual-coded papers for coefficient tables, cutoffs and external validation, on 9 for text–table conflicts and on 6 for the expression scale (κ = 0.43); the scale categories were then clarified and applied to all papers. In the blinded second coding of the text–table item, the coders agreed on 64 of 66 papers (κ = 0.73). After re-reading, three papers first coded as conflicts were recoded: one difference was rounding of the same value, one concerned AUC values rather than coefficients or counts, and one was not confirmed.
