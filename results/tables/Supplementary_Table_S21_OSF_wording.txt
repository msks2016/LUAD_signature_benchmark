Supplementary Table S21. Pre-registration wording for the replication and pooling criteria.

Source: Open Science Framework registration https://osf.io/qckvr
Original registration date: 2026-10-06
Amendment: 2026-10-10


EXCERPT 1 — Three-way per-cohort classification
(OSF: Indices §7; Inference criteria → "Three-way classification for the paper's main table")

Three-way classification per signature-cohort pair:
- Replicated: p_holm < 0.05 AND C_index > 0.5
- Contradicted: p_holm < 0.05 AND C_index < 0.5
- Inconclusive: p_holm ≥ 0.05


EXCERPT 2 — H3 descriptive specification
(OSF: Statistical models → H3 cross-cohort generalization)

H3 (cross-cohort generalization).
Per signature, C-indexes from the apparent-performance cohort (TCGA-LUAD)
and each independent cohort are compared descriptively and plotted.
No formal hypothesis test is performed at the individual-signature level,
because the number of independent cohorts per signature varies.


EXCERPT 3 — Cross-cohort pooling restriction
(OSF: Statistical models → H3 cross-cohort generalization, final sentence)

Cohorts are analyzed separately; pooling is only performed for signatures
evaluable in at least three independent cohorts and only when TCGA-LUAD
was not the development cohort for that signature.


EXCERPT 4 — Direction-consistency exploratory outcome
(OSF: Indices §9; Statistical models → "Secondary exploratory outcome, added by amendment 2026-10-10")

Direction consistency (secondary exploratory outcome, defined in the
2026-10-10 OSF amendment).
For each signature in each independent cohort: does the sign of the
HR per SD match the published sign? Tested with a binomial sign test
against a 50% null, with signatures clustered by Jaccard gene-set
overlap (>0.3 = same cluster, one observation per cluster).


APPLICATION IN THIS MANUSCRIPT

Excerpt 1 (three-way classification)
    Applied in: Methods → Discrimination metrics; Methods → Expression-matched
    null test and gene-refit divergence null.
    Used to classify Wu 2022 and Yao 2021 as Replicated in CPTAC-3, Yu 2024
    as Replicated in GSE31210, and all others as Inconclusive.

Excerpt 2 (H3 descriptive)
    Applied in: Methods → Cross-cohort synthesis; Results → Cross-cohort
    case study — Yao 2021.
    H3 is reported descriptively across the 15 signatures; no formal
    cross-cohort test was performed at the signature level.

Excerpt 3 (pooling restriction)
    Applied in: Methods → Cross-cohort synthesis; Figure 3 caption;
    Results → Cross-cohort case study — Yao 2021.
    The Yao 2021 exploratory pooled estimate reported in Figure 3 falls
    outside this rule: Yao 2021 is evaluable in two independent cohorts
    (not ≥3), and TCGA-LUAD is its development cohort. The estimate is
    therefore reported as exploratory and is not part of the pre-specified
    H3 evaluation.

Excerpt 4 (direction consistency)
    Applied in: Methods → Cross-cohort synthesis; Discussion → fourth
    paragraph.
    Reported as a secondary exploratory outcome, hypothesis-generating only.
